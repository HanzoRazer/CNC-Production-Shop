# Eco-Loom Quote Clarification

**Classification ID:** QC-ECOLOOM-2026-001  
**Date:** 2026-06-12  
**Status:** Active

---

## Purpose

This document clarifies the commercial status of Eco-Loom pricing documents prepared to date.

---

## Document Classification

The Eco-Loom production pricing documents are classified as:

**BUDGETARY PRODUCTION ESTIMATE**

This means:

- The pricing is suitable for feasibility discussion and planning.
- The pricing is not a binding formal production quote.
- Final pricing requires confirmation of outstanding requirements.

---

## What Was Previously Prepared

### Direct Manufacturing Cost Worksheet

The internal worksheet calculated direct manufacturing costs:

| Quantity | Direct Manufacturing Cost |
|----------|---------------------------|
| 100 units | $432 |
| 250 units | $1,029 |
| 500 units | $2,058 |

**These values represent direct material and machine costs only.**

They do not include:
- Risk factors
- Business overhead
- Target margin
- Finishing labor
- Packaging materials
- Shipping

### Budgetary Production Estimate

The customer-facing production estimates include risk, overhead, and margin:

| Quantity | Budgetary Estimate | Per Unit |
|----------|-------------------|----------|
| 100 units | $875 | $8.75 |
| 250 units | $2,100 | $8.40 |
| 500 units | $4,150 | $8.30 |

---

## Key Clarification

**The direct manufacturing cost values are not formal quote prices.**

The $432 / $1,029 / $2,058 figures represent internal cost calculations. They should not be interpreted as:

- Customer prices
- Accepted quotes
- Binding commitments

The budgetary production estimates ($875 / $2,100 / $4,150) are the appropriate customer-facing figures for discussion purposes.

---

## Why the Prices Differ

| Cost Layer | Description |
|------------|-------------|
| Direct Manufacturing Cost | Material + Machine time |
| Risk Factors | Tool wear, contingency, overhead, engineering recovery (40% total) |
| Target Margin | Business margin applied to risked cost (30%) |
| Budgetary Estimate | Final customer-facing budgetary figure |

The budgetary estimate is approximately 2× the direct manufacturing cost because it includes all cost layers required for sustainable production pricing.

---

## Requirements Before Formal Quote

A formal production quote requires confirmation of:

1. **Final production quantity** — Exact order quantity
2. **Finish specification** — Surface treatment requirements
3. **Packaging requirements** — How units will be packaged
4. **Delivery schedule** — When units are needed
5. **Shipping responsibility** — Who arranges and pays for shipping
6. **Quality/inspection requirements** — Acceptance criteria

Until these are confirmed, all pricing remains a budgetary estimate.
